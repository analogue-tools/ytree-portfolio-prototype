    Y TREE
    About this prototype
    One picture, more than one way to read it

    Y TREE builds a complete picture of a client’s wealth: pension, ISA, private equity, company shares, property, art. The app is how most clients navigate it, and today there is one path through that picture, organised by where each asset is held.

    A minority of clients, the most complex and least sticky, think in a different layout. This prototype adds ways to see the same wealth by what it is, by how quickly it can be reached, and by who owns it, without disturbing the view the majority rely on.

    Design principles
    • Default untouched. New views are opt-in and never replace the one clients know.
    • Calm, not gamified. It is regulated money. No streaks, no confetti.
    • One number up top, native detail underneath. Convert to a chosen currency for the overview; show the real split in the detail.
    • Every chart has its table. Numbers are never trapped in a picture.
    • Say when the next decision is due, so the client can put the phone down.
    Scope

    Fictional client, about £8.4m across the four legacy categories. Figures illustrative and reconciled across every screen. Companion to the written problem statement.
