The short version. The app already gives clients a complete picture of their wealth,
organised one way, by account. A valuable minority think about their money differently, by
liquidity, by asset class, by ownership, and today they wait for a hand-built PDF from the
client team. I kept the account view as the default and added two more perspectives and an
ownership scope on top, so the interested clients get the cut they want and everyone else
keeps the view they already know. The interface only moves where a new perspective or a
control needs the room.

What is included.

Deliverable 1, the problem, the prioritisation, and where I would start.

Deliverable 2, the prototype, and the reasoning behind each locked decision.

Live prototype at ytree-case-study.vercel.app. One fictional client, £8,420,000,
reconciled across every perspective, every owner filter, and the pivot table.

Two things to flag. All copy in the prototype is illustrative and would be aligned to
Y TREE's established client-facing voice. And I did not model tiering: which features
reach which client tier, the accountant export among them, is a commercial decision I left
out of scope and would want to work through with the team.
